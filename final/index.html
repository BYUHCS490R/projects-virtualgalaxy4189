Introduction

This website is about music from around the world. It goes over some carribean, asian, african and western music; specifically covering music from Trinidad, China, Ghana and Norway. Some of the history, background and elements of each style of music is also included on this website.



I've chosen this topic because I have been learning about different music from around the world in my music class this semester. I find it interesting how different music around the world have similarities and differences. I also find it interesting how different styles of music have come about and how music from certain places have influenced music in other parts of the world.
